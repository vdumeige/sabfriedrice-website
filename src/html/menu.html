Before you order

Peanuts. Several dishes contain peanuts or peanut sauce, including Chicken Satay, Fresh Spring Rolls, Pra Ram, Thai Style Papaya Salad and the Sab Sampler. Our kitchen also handles fish sauce, shellfish, soy, eggs and wheat. Please tell us about any allergy when you order and we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot do.

Laab Beef is served rare. Consuming raw or undercooked meats, poultry, seafood, shellfish or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness, especially if you have certain medical conditions.

Heat levels. Curries, noodle dishes and salads are made to the spice level you ask for — mild, medium, hot, or Lao hot. Prices and availability may change; call us if something matters.
